# Object Specification: Charger Plug

A two-blade (US type A) USB wall charger: a rectangular body with two parallel flat blades on one of its square faces. Its dimensions were measured from the demonstration video, scaled by the standard blade geometry; uncertainty about ±7 % on all body dimensions.

- **Body:** Rectangular block, 43.1 × 43.1 × 58.5 mm (square cross-section 43.1 × 43.1 mm; 58.5 mm along the blade axis), rounded edges (radius about 4 mm), glossy white plastic; a small port recess on the face opposite the blades.
- **Blades:** Two, each 6.35 mm wide, 1.5 mm thick, and 16 mm long, standing perpendicular to one square face with their centres 12.7 mm apart (blade faces parallel to each other, across the gap); overall length 74.5 mm along the blade axis.
- **Blade axis:** The direction from the body centre through the blades, perpendicular to the blade face. `SUCCESS.md` measures the angle between this axis and world +Z.
- **Mass:** 0.075 kg (typical for this class of adapter; not measurable from the video), centre of mass 2 mm from the body centre toward the blades.
- **Colours (cameras):** White body, dark metal-grey blades.

Surface and contact properties are not given.
