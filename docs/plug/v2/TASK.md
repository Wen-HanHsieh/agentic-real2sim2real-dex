# Task: Plug Reorientation

Reproduce the dexterous manipulation skill demonstrated in the human video using the Wuji Hand 2: **reorient a two-blade charger plug in the hand from lying on its side to standing blades-up, keep it in the hand throughout, and hold it upright at rest**.

The blade axis is the direction from the body centre through the blades. The plug starts lying on a side face in the palm with its blade axis roughly horizontal; it is upright when the blade axis is within 20° of world +Z. Nothing has to be inserted anywhere: the target has no socket.

Develop your policy in your own simulation and evaluate it in the provided target environment.
