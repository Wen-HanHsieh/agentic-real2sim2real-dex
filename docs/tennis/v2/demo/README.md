# Human Demonstration: Tennis Ball Toss and Catch

The video demonstrates the skill to reproduce with the Wuji Hand 2: toss a tennis ball from the palm, catch it, and repeat.

## Files

- **Video:** `tennis_IMG_0005_h264.mp4` (10.50 s, 30 fps, H.264, 720 × 1280).
- **Sampled frames:** `frames/` (12 frames).

One hand, palm up, holds one tennis ball and performs three consecutive vertical toss-and-catch cycles with in-hand regrasps between the tosses; the ball leaves the top of the frame near each apex. The target task requires two complete cycles with a lower apex (`SUCCESS.md`), from a fixed palm-up hand that cannot move its wrist or arm. The video illustrates the skill, not the exact target object or initial conditions.

The demonstration is for offline analysis and policy development. During target execution the deployed policy receives only the observations in `../CONTRACT.md`.
