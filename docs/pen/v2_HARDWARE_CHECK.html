Hardware Validation Tool

r2s2r-hwcheck checks a joint trajectory from any simulator against the hardware requirements of hardware/SPECS.md section 6 (hardware/hardware_spec.json, version hwspec-1.0). SuperDex judges its trials with the same code, so a trajectory that passes here passes the same rules on the target. It needs only numpy and does not depend on a simulator, learning method or controller.

0. The rules (copy of hardware/SPECS.md section 6)

These rules are the single hardware specification used by SuperDex to judge trials, by the sensor-based feedback (FEEDBACK.md) and by the hardware validation tool (HARDWARE_CHECK.md). Machine-readable copy: hardware/hardware_spec.json (version hwspec-1.0).

QuantityRequirement (every joint, every step)Notes
Joint positionlower − 0.02 ≤ q ≤ upper + 0.02 rad (URDF limits, section 2)Joint stops are compliant: a joint that is pressed into its stop (by a target at or beyond the limit, by contact or by inertia) deflects slightly past the nominal URDF value. The allowance keeps pressing into a stop compliant; travel further past a stop is a violation.
Joint velocity`q̇≤` URDF max speed (section 2), no allowanceApplies to joints back-driven by contact.
Joint torque`τ_applied≤` URDF effort (section 2), no allowanceThe actuator is current-limited: the PD torque kp (q_target - q) + kd (qd_target - qd) is saturated at the effort limit, so the applied torque never exceeds it on the target. A PD demand above the limit is saturation; it is reported, and it is not a violation.
Actionsq_target: shape (20,), finite; qd_target: None or shape (20,), finiteTargets outside the URDF limits are clipped. A non-finite or wrongly shaped action, a policy error, or no answer within 5 s ends the trial.

Nothing else is enforced on the hardware. The torque–speed curve is not published; the applied torque never exceeds the effort limit on the target, so a policy cannot violate the torque requirement there — check it in your own simulator with HARDWARE_CHECK.md if your actuator model can exceed it.

1. Usage

r2s2r-hwcheck traj.npz                 # simulator state (exact)
r2s2r-hwcheck traj.npz --measured      # noisy encoder readings (sigma from SPECS.md section 5)
r2s2r-hwcheck traj.npz --json report.json
python tools/hwcheck/hwcheck.py traj.npz --spec hardware/hardware_spec.json   # the same tool from this bundle

Exit code 0 unless a violation or an invalid action is found.

2. Input (.npz, one row per sample)

KeyShapeNeeded for
q(n, 20) radposition (required)
qd(n, 20) rad/svelocity
tau_applied(n, 20) N·mtorque: the torque your actuator model applied (not the PD demand)
q_target, qd_target(n, 20)action validity; PD torque demand (estimate)
t(n,) ssample spacing (or --dt)
joint_names(20,)column order (default: URDF order, as in hardware/SPECS.md)

Sample at your physics rate (e.g. 1 kHz) to see every PD tick.

3. Report

Each check is reported with how it was obtained:

  • simulated (exact): your simulator's state; a value beyond a limit is a violation.
  • measured (noisy) with --measured: violation if a value, or the mean of 10 or 50 consecutive values, lies beyond a limit by more than k·σ/√window; compliant if every value lies inside by more than k·σ; otherwise uncertain (k makes a wrong clear decision at most 1 % likely). Torque is not measurable.
  • estimated: the PD torque demand kp (q_target − q) + kd (qd_target − qd) from the published gains. A demand above the effort limit is saturation; it is reported, and it is not a violation.
  • not checked: inputs you did not provide.

Overall status: violation, compliant, uncertain (measured data within noise of a limit) or incomplete (inputs missing).
