# Human Demonstration: Pen Spinning

The video demonstrates the skill to reproduce with the Wuji Hand 2: spin a pen around the fingers.

## Files

- **Video:** `penspinning_first6s.mp4` (6.00 s, 30 fps).
- **Sampled frames:** `frames/` (12 frames).

The pen in the video differs from the target pen (`object/SPECS.md`), and the video carries an on-screen caption unrelated to the task. The video illustrates the skill, not the exact target object or initial conditions.

The demonstration is for offline analysis and policy development. During target execution the deployed policy receives only the observations in `../CONTRACT.md`.
