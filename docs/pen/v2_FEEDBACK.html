Feedback Protocol v2: Cameras, Sensor Verifier, Records

Task: Pen Spinning. Everything in this document describes data returned by fetch, frames and history (REMOTE_API.md). The policy's observation and action interface (CONTRACT.md) is unchanged.

1. Cameras

Three fixed, hardware-synchronized RGB-D cameras (global shutter, 848 × 480, pinhole without distortion, D405-class depth). All three views show the same instant in frame k.

ViewPurpose
topfrom above: contacts on the palm side, headings, rotations about +Z, the cube's U face
leftfrom the hand's left (+Y), nearly level: heights, toss trajectory, grasp and fingers
rightfrom the hand's right (−Y) and the fingertip side, elevated: complementary view for 3D pose

calibration.json gives, per view, K (3×3), T_world_cam (camera → world) and T_cam_world, plus:

  • World frame: hardware/SPECS.md (X forward, Y left, Z up, metres).
  • Camera frame: OpenCV (x right, y down, z forward); pixel (0, 0) is the centre of the top-left pixel.
  • Time: t in seconds since control step 0; control_step = round(t × 500); video/frames.json lists both per frame.
  • Depth: z along the optical axis; 16-bit PNG in 0.1 mm units in frame queries, 0 = no return; noise σ = 1 mm × (z / 0.5 m)².
  • Video: 100 fps, H.264, real time, covering the whole policy phase.

2. Sensor-based verifier (sensor/sensor_report.json)

A deterministic program scores the trial against SUCCESS.md using only the camera frames, your robot log (logs/trial_0.npz), the public robot model and mount (hardware/) and the object's public dimensions (object/SPECS.md). It never reads simulator state. Fields:

FieldMeaning
statussuccess, failure or unknown (rule in status_rule); task_status: the same for the task alone
score, score_boundstotal progress 0–1 (SUCCESS.md stages: spin-up, sustain, hold); the interval covers moments that could not be observed and measurement error near thresholds
progress, reward (+ _bounds)the same quantity as score: the official per-trial progress and reward
confidence0–1: observed fraction × (1 − interval width)
stagesper-stage score and score bounds; every milestone: reached (point), reached_bounds, certain, time, label
unobservablequantities in SUCCESS.md that the sensors cannot observe for this task, and what that means for the score
eventstimestamped observations (release, landing, rest, rotation milestones, layer turns, loss of retention, ...)
object_motionmeasured summary quantities (see §3)
hardwaremeasured (joint positions and speeds vs the URDF limits) and estimated (torque demand from the PD law)
runtimesteps run vs expected, finite actions, policy errors, inference time
observabilityframes, visibility fractions, margins used (tolerances), and what is not observable
version, rubric_version, rig_version, code_sha256exact versions; identical inputs give identical reports

Status rule: success only if the score is 1.0 even when every unobservable or near-threshold moment counts against the policy, hardware is compliant and the run is valid; failure if the score is below 1.0 even when they all count for it, or a hardware limit is measurably exceeded, or the run is invalid; otherwise unknown.

The verifier states what happened, never why. It is an independent sensor reading, not the cell's internal judgement: official success remains as defined in SUCCESS.md (score 1.0 with every physical, hardware and runtime requirement in every required trial), and the cell's internal validity checks have no sensor equivalent.

Hardware

Judged by the authoritative rules of hardware/SPECS.md section 6 (hardware/hardware_spec.json) with the same code as HARDWARE_CHECK.md, applied to your robot log:

  • Measured: joint positions (against the URDF limits with the published 0.02 rad allowance) and joint speeds. Each gets violation, compliant or uncertain from the documented encoder noise (σ 0.002 rad, 0.05 rad/s): violation if a measured value, or the mean of 10 or 50 consecutive values, lies beyond a limit by more than k·σ/√window; compliant if every value lies inside by more than k·σ; otherwise uncertain. A clear violation makes the trial status failure; an uncertain decision makes it at best unknown.
  • Commanded: action validity (finite, correctly shaped; targets outside the limits are clipped).
  • Estimated: torque demand. Nothing measures torque on this hand; estimated.joint_torque_demand applies the PD law of hardware/SPECS.md to the measured state and your targets. Above the effort limit the actuator saturates: reported, not a violation (the applied torque never exceeds the limit on the target).

Not observable: the cell's internal validity checks; contact forces; joint torque; for the pen, its rotation about the long axis while both roll markers are hidden (then only a lower bound on its angular speed is known). An object hidden from all three cameras for longer than 0.5 s is treated as not retained in the point estimate and as unknown in the interval. The point score credits only what was observed; score_bounds gives the range.

sensor/sensor_tracks.npz holds the per-frame tracks: t, hand_contact, environment_contact, hand_contact_uncertain, environment_contact_uncertain, hand_gap_m, and pen_axis (n,3) unit vector back->tip, pen_centre (n,3) [m], pen_speed (n,) [m/s], pen_swing_rate (n,) [rad/s]. NaN means not observed.

4. Segmentation masks (masks/)

For every frame and view, the object's pixels in that camera image, derived from the cameras: the object's camera-estimated 3D pose rendered with its public geometry, kept where the observed depth agrees with the model surface (within 8 mm). Pixels hidden behind something nearer (fingers) are not part of the mask. Masks are pixel-aligned with the RGB and depth images of the same view and share video/frames.json timestamps.

masks/<view>.npz: rle_counts, rle_offsets (mask k = run lengths counts[offsets[k]:offsets[k+1]] over the row-major H × W image, alternating background/object, starting with background), frame_index, t, control_step, valid (a camera pose estimate existed), area, model_area (unoccluded silhouette), visible_fraction, colour_agreement, occluded_px, shape, label. The masks command returns selected frames as PNG.

6. Frame queries

frames sNN <view|all> <t1,...> [depth] returns PNG frames rendered by the same cameras at any instant of the trial (nearest 1 ms), with frames/frames.json giving the exact time and control step. Use it to inspect moments between video frames, to get depth, or to check events reported by the verifier.

7. Experiment records

history keeps an immutable record of every submission (package, archive and feedback SHA-256; state; sensor summary) and your note entries (hypothesis → change → evidence → decision, diagnostic or improvement, with your own local-simulation result if you provide one), so you can compare local predictions with SuperDex observations across iterations. Feedback bundles of finished submissions never change.

Public research page. Agents run on AWS and interact with the SuperDex target only through its submission API. SuperDex is a black-box target environment treated as the real system in this setup; it is not a physical robot deployment. Official results come from the sensor verifier sv-2.2 with hardware specification hwspec-1.0; privileged simulator state and the operator's ground-truth comparison are never published or returned to agents.
