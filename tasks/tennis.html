      Bundle tools · agent_tools/

      • bin/r2s2r-calibSim-to-target calibration (required after every target evaluation with feedback, TOOLS.md section 3): extract, replay, compare, diagnose, video, record. Local only: nothing is sent anywhere.
      • bin/r2s2r-harnessResearch harness client: experiment records, best policy, regression gate (bookkeeping only).
      • bin/r2s2r-hwcheckCheck a joint trajectory against the Wuji Hand 2 hardware rules (hardware/SPECS.md section 7). Local only: nothing is sent anywhere.
      • bin/r2s2r-preflightLocal pre-check of a policy package for the target environment (see /usr/local/lib/r2s2r/preflight.py). Nothing is sent anywhere.
      • calib.pySim-to-target calibration (local, simulator-independent): compare your own simulation with a target rollout.
      • calib_adapter.pySimulator adapter interface of r2s2r-calib: how your own simulator is driven during a replay.
      • calib_diagnose.pyDiagnosis of a target-vs-simulation comparison (r2s2r-calib diagnose): contact-phase segmentation, error growth per segment, and an attribution of the discrepancies to the wrist controller / finger actuator model, free object motion or contact phases WHERE THE EVIDENCE SUPPORTS IT, with an explicit "cannot determine" otherwise.
      • calib_kinematic_adapter.pyKinematic reference adapter for r2s2r-calib (NumPy only; no physics).
      • calib_mujoco_adapter.pyExample simulator adapter for r2s2r-calib built on MuJoCo (a real dynamics model; needs `pip install mujoco`).
      • harness.pyResearch harness client: experiment records, best-policy preservation, regression gate (bookkeeping only).
      • preflight.pyr2s2r-preflight: local pre-check of a policy package (contract v2) for the target environment.
