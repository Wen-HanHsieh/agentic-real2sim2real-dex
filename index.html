Autonomous Dexterous Manipulation from Black-box Target Feedback

Autonomous research agents build their own simulations, train dexterous-hand policies and improve them from the ground-truth feedback of a black-box target.

Read the abstract

An autonomous research agent receives a human demonstration video, a task description, the success criteria, and the robot and object specifications. From these it builds its own training simulator, designs its reward and training recipe, and trains a policy for a dexterous hand on a free-floating wrist that observes the exact state of the object. It submits the policy to a black-box target that executes one trial and returns the official result of its ground-truth verifier together with the exact state record, multi-view video and joint logs, but never its physics parameters or the reason for a failure. After each target evaluation the agent calibrates its simulator against the target rollout (replaying the target's actions in its own simulator) before its next submission — or records a documented calibration exception when the feedback is unavailable or sealed or replay is infeasible — diagnoses the result, changes its simulator, reward or policy, and resubmits. Four dexterous manipulation tasks, one agent per task, 100 target submissions each; this page is the live record of the experiment.

Method

Framework

Task specification → autonomous agent → simulation and policy development → black-box target → ground-truth feedback → sim-to-target calibration (required after every evaluation) → iterative improvement. The agent never sees the target's physics parameters, simulator or verifier code; it learns from the official result and the exact rollout record the target returns.

Task specification human · fixed

A human demonstration video of the skill to reproduce, a task description, the success criteria, and the robot and object specifications.

Autonomous agent agent · modifiable

Owns its training simulator, reward and training recipe, and the policy. After each target result it must replay the target's actions in its own simulator (sim-to-target calibration; a documented exception only when feedback is unavailable or sealed or replay is infeasible), compare the trajectories, form a hypothesis, change what it owns, retrain and resubmit.

Black-box target target · fixed

Executes one trial per submission and scores the exact state record with its ground-truth verifier against the published criteria. It returns the official result, the state record, multi-view video and joint logs; its physics parameters stay hidden. A technically invalid rollout (policy execution failure, hardware or run-check violation) is reported with its validity findings but never ranked; a physically failed but valid rollout is ranked with its actual progress.

      Bundle tools · agent_tools/

      • bin/r2s2r-calibSim-to-target calibration (required after every target evaluation with feedback, TOOLS.md section 3): extract, replay, compare, diagnose, video, record. Local only: nothing is sent anywhere.
      • bin/r2s2r-harnessResearch harness client: experiment records, best policy, regression gate (bookkeeping only).
      • bin/r2s2r-hwcheckCheck a joint trajectory against the Wuji Hand 2 hardware rules (hardware/SPECS.md section 7). Local only: nothing is sent anywhere.
      • bin/r2s2r-preflightLocal pre-check of a policy package for the target environment (see /usr/local/lib/r2s2r/preflight.py). Nothing is sent anywhere.
      • calib.pySim-to-target calibration (local, simulator-independent): compare your own simulation with a target rollout.
      • calib_adapter.pySimulator adapter interface of r2s2r-calib: how your own simulator is driven during a replay.
      • calib_diagnose.pyDiagnosis of a target-vs-simulation comparison (r2s2r-calib diagnose): contact-phase segmentation, error growth per segment, and an attribution of the discrepancies to the wrist controller / finger actuator model, free object motion or contact phases WHERE THE EVIDENCE SUPPORTS IT, with an explicit "cannot determine" otherwise.
      • calib_kinematic_adapter.pyKinematic reference adapter for r2s2r-calib (NumPy only; no physics).
      • calib_mujoco_adapter.pyExample simulator adapter for r2s2r-calib built on MuJoCo (a real dynamics model; needs `pip install mujoco`).
      • harness.pyResearch harness client: experiment records, best-policy preservation, regression gate (bookkeeping only).
      • preflight.pyr2s2r-preflight: local pre-check of a policy package (contract v2) for the target environment.
