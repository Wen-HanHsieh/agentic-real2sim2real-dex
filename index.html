Agentic Real2Sim2Real: Closed-loop Feedback Learning

Autonomous coding agents learn dexterous manipulation skills for the 20-joint Wuji Hand 2 from a single human video. Each agent builds its own simulation, trains a policy, submits it to the black-box SuperDex target and improves from sensor-based feedback: three camera views, the robot's own logs and an official evaluation computed only from what the cameras observe.

Research status: autonomous research under this setting has not started. The infrastructure (sv-2.2, hwspec-1.0) has been validated; the trials under Validation are infrastructure tests, not research results.

1 · Real Robot / SuperDex

Executes the submitted policy and collects observable execution data.

  • Multi-view RGB-D observations from three fixed, calibrated, synchronized cameras (Top / Left / Right).
  • Robot joint state history (q, qd) and action history (q_target, qd_target) at 500 Hz.
  • Execution results, runtime status and hardware enforcement under the published specification (hwspec-1.0).

SuperDex is the black-box target environment, treated as the real system in this research setup. It is not a physical robot deployment.

2 · Real-side Evaluation Harness

Produces the official task evaluation from sensor-observable data only: the cameras, the robot's own logs and the public documents.

  • Success / Failure / Unknown, task progress and reward (the same 0–1 quantity), stage scores and milestones.
  • Task-specific metrics and events; hardware diagnostics (measured joint positions and speeds, estimated torque demand).
  • Measurement confidence and uncertainty bounds; a milestone that cannot be decided is marked uncertain, not guessed.
  • Sensor-derived object tracks and segmentation masks for every frame and view.

Official verifier sv-2.2, hardware specification hwspec-1.0. It runs sandboxed and never reads privileged simulator state; an operator-only ground-truth comparison measures its accuracy and is never returned to agents.

3 · Feedback to Agent

Makes the observable evidence and the official evaluation available to the agent after the trial.

  • RGB videos of all three views, on-demand RGB-D frames, robot joint states and actions.
  • Official success / failure / unknown, progress / reward, metrics, milestones, events, hardware diagnostics and uncertainty.
  • Sensor-derived object tracks and segmentation masks.

Only submission metadata comes back with submit; the agent polls status and pulls everything else (table below). There is no live feedback during a trial, and the policy never receives camera images.

4 · Agent + Research Harness (AWS)

The agent does the research; the harness keeps records and checks them.

  • Agent: failure analysis → hypothesis → local simulation development and refinement → policy training and improvement → experiment planning and resubmission. It chooses its own simulators, algorithms, learning methods, training rewards and optimization strategies.
  • Research Harness: experiment history and append-only, hash-chained notes; exact submitted packages (package) and checkpoint references; candidate / best-policy tracking; regression comparison against the best comparable earlier submission; hardware validation (r2s2r-hwcheck); optional Sim-to-Real mask IoU (r2s2r-maskiou).

The harness measures and records. Its regression gate labels submissions (improved / equal / regressed / not comparable); it does not retrain, choose strategies or change official results. Records are kept by SuperDex, outside the agent's reach, and read from AWS through the API and r2s2r-harness.

Known limitations

  • Pen success is certified only when the camera evidence is sufficient; otherwise the verifier reports unknown rather than guessing. Some successful pen trials can therefore come back as unknown.
  • Contact is inferred from camera geometry and the measured joint angles; gaps near the contact threshold are treated as uncertain and widen the score interval.
  • Joint positions and speeds are measured; joint torque is an estimate from the public gains.
  • A segmentation mask is marked invalid in frames where the object's pose cannot be estimated from the cameras.
  • Depth is available through frame queries only; the videos are RGB.
  • Feedback is available after the trial only; there are no live queries during a running trial.

Vision queries as function callsadaptedFrame and mask queries after the rollout (any view, any instant). Live queries would change the 500 Hz policy interface, so they are not offered.
Hard limitsadaptedOne published hardware specification, enforced by SuperDex, reported by the verifier and checkable by the agent with r2s2r-hwcheck.
Held-out evaluationadoptedVerifier development and acceptance used separate trial sets; the operator's ground-truth comparison stays private.

Learning algorithms, policies, planners, controllersomittedNot imported: agents keep full autonomy over methods.
