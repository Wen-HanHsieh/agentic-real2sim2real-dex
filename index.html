TASK.md

Task: ball toss, catch and spin

Episode: 6.0 s. The hand is mounted palm up with a baseball resting in it (target/screenshot.png). Goal, in order:

  1. toss the ball at least 5 cm above its release point;
  2. catch it in the hand and bring it to rest;
  3. spin it in the hand by at least 720° counterclockwise, viewed from above, about the world vertical axis +Z;
  4. hold it at rest in the hand through the last 0.3 s.

Rotation is the signed cumulative angular displacement of the ball about world +Z after the catch: clockwise subtracts, rotation about horizontal axes does not count. The ball's size, mass and surface properties are not given; its red seams make its rotation visible. Skill demonstration: demo/. Pass and fail conditions: SUCCESS.md.

SUCCESS.md

Success criteria: ball toss, catch and spin

1. Horizon

6.0 s = 3000 policy steps at 500 Hz; all steps always run. Times are measured from step 0.

2. Success (all stages, in this order, in every required episode)

StageCondition
Tossa release at which the ball leaves the hand moving upward, followed by ≥ 0.10 s without any contact, an apex ≥ 5 cm above the release point, and a free-fall arc. The first release meeting all of these is the toss; earlier hops and regrasps are allowed
Catchthe toss's flight ends on the hand; afterwards the ball touches nothing but the hand; it then comes to rest: speed < 0.02 m/s continuously for 0.1 s while touching the hand. The first such moment is t_rest
Spinfrom t_rest to the end: net rotation ∫ ω·ẑ dt ≥ +720° about world +Z (counterclockwise from above is positive); contact interruptions < 0.10 s; the centre stays within 3 cm of its position at t_rest
Holdduring the last 0.3 s: the running net rotation is ≥ +720° throughout (reached before the hold begins, never lost); the centre's speed is < 0.02 m/s; the ball touches the hand at the last step

3. Failure

Any of: no release meets the toss conditions (public reasons: "object moving downward at release", "contact-free flight < 0.10 s", "apex < 5 cm above release", "motion before or after release not consistent with a hand-driven free-fall toss"); the ball is still in flight at the end; it lands on anything but the hand; it touches anything but the hand after landing; it never comes to rest; net rotation < +720°; displacement > 3 cm; a contact gap ≥ 0.10 s; rest reached less than 0.3 s before the end; rotation < +720° or speed ≥ 0.02 m/s at any time in the last 0.3 s; not in the hand at the end; any validity violation (§4).

4. Validity (every step of every episode)

RequirementRule
hardware_limitsjoint positions within the URDF limits (tolerance 0.02 rad); joint speed ≤ max speed and joint torque ≤ effort (hardware/SPECS.md) on all 20 joints, no tolerance, back-driven joints included
valid_actionsevery action finite with the right shape; the policy loads, resets and answers each act within 5 s
run_validitythe package passes the code check (PACKAGE.md); the ball is at rest in the hand at step 0; the ball's motion is consistent with contact and gravity (no penetration beyond the cell's tolerance, no unexplained jumps)

5. Determinism and evaluation

  • Required episodes: nominal_0, nominal_1, robustness_0 (an undisclosed condition). Success = all three pass and determinism holds. Phase 1 also runs sealed robustness episodes that do not affect the result.
  • Every episode of one evaluation uses the same encoder-noise realization, fixed by the package and the phase. act must be a deterministic function of the observations since reset(); the two nominal episodes must produce bit-identical commands. Each episode runs in a fresh policy process.
  • Feedback exists in phase 2 only: per episode, pass/fail and a reason per requirement, the task metrics, peak speed and torque ratios per joint, the observation and command log, and video of nominal_0 (REMOTE_API.md).

Task: pen spinning

Episode: 12.0 s. The hand is mounted palm up with a pen lying across its fingers (target/screenshot.png). Goal:

  1. spin the whole pen around the fingers, end over end, for at least 3 complete revolutions (+1080°) counterclockwise, viewed from above, about the world vertical axis +Z, each revolution within 3.0 s of the previous one;

SUCCESS.md

Success criteria: pen spinning

1. Horizon

12.0 s = 6000 policy steps at 500 Hz; all steps always run. Times are measured from step 0.

2. Success (every required episode)

Rotation = signed cumulative change, since step 0, of the heading of the pen's tip direction (back end to red tip) projected on the horizontal plane, about world +Z; counterclockwise from above is positive. Rotation about the pen's own long axis leaves the heading unchanged and does not count; clockwise motion subtracts, so back-and-forth motion nets to zero. Any motion that turns the heading counts, however it is produced.

RequirementCondition
spinthe rotation reaches +1080° (3 revolutions). With t1, t2, t3 the first times it reaches +360°, +720°, +1080°: t1 ≤ 3.0 s, t2 − t1 ≤ 3.0 s, t3 − t2 ≤ 3.0 s
in_handfrom step 0 to the end: the pen touches nothing but the hand; contact interruptions < 0.10 s; the centre stays within 8 cm horizontally of its position at step 0 and never drops more than 3 cm below it; the long axis never tilts more than 45° from horizontal
final_holdthroughout the last 1.0 s: rotation ≥ +1080° (the third revolution completed before the hold, never undone); centre speed < 0.02 m/s; angular speed < 0.5 rad/s; touching the hand at every moment

3. Failure

Any of: fewer than 3 revolutions; a revolution taking more than 3.0 s after the previous one (the first, after step 0); contact with anything but the hand; a contact gap ≥ 0.10 s; horizontal travel > 8 cm; drop > 3 cm; tilt > 45°; during the last 1.0 s rotation < +1080°, centre speed ≥ 0.02 m/s, angular speed ≥ 0.5 rad/s or loss of hand contact; any validity violation (§4).

TASK.md

Task: one-handed 2×2×2 Rubik's cube solving

Episode: 40.0 s. The hand is mounted palm up with a scrambled 2×2×2 cube resting in its cupped hand (target/screenshot.png). Goal: solve the cube by turning its layers with this hand only, keep it in the hand throughout, and hold it solved through the last 1.0 s.

  • Cube: 8 corner cubies; a layer of 4 cubies turns about one of the three axes through the centre, and a layer can turn only while the other layers are aligned. Solved: every face shows one colour and no layer is part-way through a turn, in any orientation of the whole cube. Size, mass, turning resistance and surface properties are not given. Solved colours: U white, D yellow, F green, B blue, R red, L orange.
  • Start orientation (each face within about 15°): U faces world +Z (up), F faces the fingertips (world −X), R faces world −Y (little-finger side), L the thumb side, D the palm, B the wrist. Face names are fixed by this start orientation; the cube may be reoriented freely afterwards.
  • Moves (WCA): X = 90° clockwise looking at face X from outside, X2 = 180°, X' = 90° counterclockwise. A 2×2×2 has no fixed centres: L equals R' plus a whole-cube rotation; likewise D = U' and B = F'.
  • Scramble: random per evaluation, a legal state exactly 3 face turns from solved (half-turn metric), given to the policy in every observation as obs["scramble"] (CONTRACT.md).

Skill demonstration: demo/. Pass and fail conditions: SUCCESS.md.
