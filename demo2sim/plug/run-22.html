Plug manipulation verification criteria, version 1

Evidence and scope

The 30 Hz video and reconstruction r0002 show two sequential cycles. Deployment uses one white station plug. The white cycle is the primary reference, and the black cycle confirms the order and the change in wrist and finger pose. Frame numbers below are zero-based. Measurements come from given/recon/analysis/reference_states.npz and the phase labels in given/recon/analysis/analysis.json.

The fitted contact timings and precise forces are uncertain. The criteria use simulation contacts and measured robot motion where the video cannot resolve those details.

Ordered stages

  1. Lift. Begin with the plug supported on its deployment face. Make thumb plus opposing-finger contact with the palm-side normal within 45° of world down and the plug still supported or just lifting. Then keep an opposed grip off the table for at least 0.10 s, rising at least 10 mm above initial centre height within that grip. The white grasp was about 7° from down. The 45° tolerance allows approach adjustments within the robot's reachable workspace and preserves an above-table approach. The 10 mm rise is below the measured 25 mm airborne peak and clears initial contact and small support-pose errors. The 0.10 s interval is the shortest counted by the standard measurement and excludes momentary collision. Standard predicates are opposed_s >= 0.10 and max_rise_m >= 0.010.
  2. Turn. Continue in an opposed, unsupported grip. The object must rotate at least 50° relative to the palm before reaching blades within 20° of up in that grip. The peak wrist rotation within the successful opposed interval must be at most 65°, close to the observed 45° white/54° black swing with allowance for robot reach and monocular uncertainty. The 50° threshold preserves over half of the white demonstration's 88° relative turn despite the changed start heading and uncertain occluded pose. The 20° approach tolerance permits final upright correction during descent. Over the episode, the standard nearly-still-joint indicator may accumulate at most 25° passive relative rotation, half of the required relative turn. During the successful turn, require at least 35° of relative excursion accompanied by actual finger motion, estimated by composing relative-rotation increments when the 0.10 s mean norm of finger-joint speeds is at least 0.30 rad/s (the harness indicator). This is a motion proxy and can include contact rolling. Standard predicates are opposed_rel_rotation_deg >= 50, opposed_wrist_rotation_deg <= 65, and passive_rel_rotation_deg <= 25.
  3. Carry and release. With the same opposed grip after turning, enter the goal zone with blades within 20° of up while the plug is airborne. It must have moved at least 0.15 m toward +y from start before that entry. The white and black demonstrations travel 0.199 m and 0.212 m respectively, and the station's nearest permissible goal boundary is 0.25 m from the nominal start. Moving left may overlap the turn. Descend onto table support with hand contact and blades within 20° of vertical, then break contact while the plug is supported. This permits continuous support transfer and excludes dropping an airborne plug into the zone. Use the evaluator's ordered contact, position and axis checks plus standard touching_hand_at_end <= 0 and touching_environment_at_end >= 1.
  4. Placed. Apply the following exact condition over the final 0.5 s, using the object state-frame origin and standing height of -0.2935 m. Standard predicates also check final position, axis and contact. Evaluation verifies the full continuous sampled end window.

Success condition (given; include it unchanged in your criteria):

  • the plug's body centre is in the goal zone, x in [0.28, 0.48] m and y in [-0.10, 0.05] m;
  • it stands blades up: its +Z axis is within 10 deg of vertical;
  • its centre is at its standing height on the table within +-10 mm;
  • it is at rest: linear speed below 0.02 m/s and angular speed below 0.5 rad/s;
  • all of this holds over the last 0.5 s of the episode, and the hand does not touch it then.

Judge only episodes that satisfy all stages in order with no stop and no hardware violation. Attempts can restart after a missed grasp or drop. Carry and release must follow a qualifying lift and turn on the same attempt. The task suite requires at least 80% successful episodes across independent fixed-start position and heading draws and sensor seeds. This is the mastery requirement, allowing some failed episodes under the noisy sensor. Zero hardware violations are allowed. The feasibility suite requires at least 25% across the identical starts and criteria to demonstrate a physical path without making a robustness claim. The verification tests the single deployment plug and does not establish performance for the two-plug sequence or on hardware.
