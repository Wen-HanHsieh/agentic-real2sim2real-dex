Verification criteria v1 — own simulation claim

Evidence is the human video and the unchanged reconstruction r0002 (version 1). The reconstruction's analysis/quantification.json is the quantitative source; metric depth, hidden contacts and anatomical poses are estimates, not ground truth.

The hand approaches the housing palm down, closes thumb against index/middle, lifts clear of the desk, turns the housing actively between fingers, carries it left, lowers it onto its USB end, releases and withdraws. White contact is 2.43–7.03 s; black contact 8.33–12.80 s. White/black turn intervals are 3.67–4.77 / 10.30–10.87 s. Measured object rotation relative to the wrist during those turns is 87.6/74.3 degrees, with wrist rotation 27.1/17.3 degrees. Whole contact relative rotation reaches 81.9/112.0 degrees. Thumb/index/middle tip excursions in the turn are 36/54/68 mm and 29/33/38 mm. Object centre rises 24/34 mm and horizontal displacement is 205/212 mm. Airborne clearance of 10 mm is an explicit inferred depth prior. Grasp palm-normal tilt from down is 6/39 degrees; turn tilt 26/64; carry tilt 41/54. Final upright orientation was constrained using visible support, rather than independently resolved to 0.01 degree. Phase boundaries are uncertain by 3–5 frames.

Deployment has one random kind, arbitrary heading and either broad or narrow side down in the entire specified start rectangle. All criteria apply to that object and its own phases, without matching a fixed video trajectory. Timing may stretch to accommodate this arm's delays and reach. Retries are allowed.

Ordered stages:

  1. grasp: contact the housing with thumb and another finger. The hand has contact for at least 0.10 s (hand_contact_s >= 0.10). At first sustained grasp the palm's +X normal is within 65 degrees of down. The tilt tolerance covers the demonstrated black grasp's 47-degree upper quantile plus uncertainty and up to 15 degrees adaptation to the robot workspace.
  2. lift: an opposed grip clear of all environment contacts for at least 0.25 s (opposed_s >= 0.25) and centre rise at least 15 mm (max_rise_m >= 0.015). Additionally at least one opposed interval has lowest object surface 5 mm above the tabletop. The centre-rise threshold is below both measured rises because the video depth is inferred with millimetre-scale relative uncertainty; surface clearance distinguishes lifting from rolling on the table.
  3. turn: within one continuous opposed grip, at least 60 degrees rotation relative to the palm (opposed_rel_rotation_deg >= 60), at most 20 degrees passive rotation (passive_rel_rotation_deg <= 20), and finger joint excursion norm at least 0.40 rad during that turn. Sixty degrees is below both measured active turns by 14–28 degrees, consistent with uncertain hidden orientation. The passive bound is an explicit conservative interpretation of the instruction to turn with fingers: most turning must happen while fingers move. During the qualifying turn, net palm rotation is at most 60 degrees and median palm-normal tilt at most 75 degrees. These cover both measured turns and adaptation, while excluding a wrist-only rigid-grasp solution. There is no table-supported pivot or free-flight throw in this stage. The passive-rotation metric is only an indicator of causality; moving fingers could still permit slipping.
  4. carry: after the qualifying turn, the opposed-held object's centre enters x [0.28, 0.48], y [-0.10, 0.05], with blade +Z within 20 degrees of up. At least 0.10 s of this state must occur without environment contact. The tolerance gives room for subsequent precision placement; the final criterion below remains 10 degrees. Standard predicate held_s >= 0.35.
  5. place: the given success condition below holds at the episode end. Custom state-log checks evaluate every logged row over the last 0.5 s. Standard predicates additionally check final x/y bounds, vertical +Z within 10 degrees, no hand contact and environment contact at the end.
Success condition (given; include it unchanged in your criteria):
- the plug's body centre is in the goal zone, x in [0.28, 0.48] m and y in [-0.10, 0.05] m;
- it stands blades up: its +Z axis is within 10 deg of vertical;
- its centre is at its standing height on the table within +-10 mm;
- it is at rest: linear speed below 0.02 m/s and angular speed below 0.5 rad/s;
- all of this holds over the last 0.5 s of the episode, and the hand does not touch it then.

An episode succeeds only if all five stages pass in order, the hardware rules hold throughout, and no stop occurs. Full episodes last 60 s at 30 Hz after the simulated 1.5 s ramp. The task pass rate is 0.80, the prescribed mastery level, evaluated on at least 64 fresh episodes; report Wilson intervals and all failures. The matching feasibility suite requires 0.25, with the same starts and criteria, using any controller. Zero hardware-violation episodes are allowed. The fixed local development seeds are 1000–1063.

Assumptions: exact charger brand is unresolved; reconstruction body-centre frames equal deployment frames. A 180-degree blade-axis symmetry may be ignored for grasp selection, but +Z direction cannot. The robot has longer, different fingers than MANO, so geometric finger contacts and active turning replace direct joint-angle imitation. Placement location follows the stated robot-frame goal zone, not the camera image's far left. Broad tolerance in wrist yaw is needed for heading adaptation; downward-facing palm and active in-hand turn remain mandatory. All results are claims about this simulator, with no real-robot success claim or target submission in this run.
