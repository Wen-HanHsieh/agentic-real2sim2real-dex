Agent verification criteria — version 1

This defines my own simulation claim for D2S-PLUG-7. There is no official task score. Recorded before training or policy evaluation. Reconstruction version 5 (record r0012) is the reference. TASK.md requires white first, then black, each picked from the right and stood on the left with blades up. The task goal has priority over exact motion copying; wrist resemblance is measured explicitly.

The reference is the recorded reconstruction, all 436 frames at 30 Hz. Event boundaries are manually estimated to within three frames. The measured values and exact palm-frame definition are in skill_measurements.json. Absolute monocular depth is uncertain: calibration sensitivity is about 76 mm globally. Relative desk transport, support, visible axial direction and timing are more strongly constrained. Occluded poses and missing hand-fit frames are not treated as ground truth.

Ordered task plan and measurements

  1. Approach and grasp white (frames 37–99; contact begins 71).
  2. Lift and rotate white (100–140), then carry left (141–159).
  3. Place white on its base (160), release by 212, and leave it standing.
  4. Return to black (213–253), grasp it (254–279).
  5. Lift and rotate black (280–332), carry left (333–359).
  6. Place black on its base (360), release by 384, and withdraw (385–435).

These are physical subgoals, not a prescribed controller. Visual evidence directly supports color order, contact/release, leftward travel and final blade direction. Full pose during occlusion, exact forces, friction and mass are inferred or supplied by the object specification.

Quantities and pass conditions

All positions refer to body-center state frames, not COM. Tabletop is z=0, +Y is left, and a left-side object has body-center y>0.15 m as specified in TASK_CONFIG.md. Use one fixed reconstruction-to-scene transform declared before training. It aligns the table and initial scene; no alignment is fitted to evaluation rollouts.

An episode succeeds only if all of the following hold in order:

  • Both objects start sideways on the right. White is manipulated first. Black center displacement must remain <=5 mm and its rotation drift <=5 deg until white has been released upright on the left for at least 0.5 s. The video leaves black stationary while white is handled;5 mm/5 deg accommodate contact settling and pose uncertainty. The observed release-to-next-grasp gap is about 1.37 s;0.5 s is a conservative settling interval, several gravitational settling times for a 6 cm body.
  • Each charger must be lifted: its lowest collision-surface point must exceed 10 mm (white) or 30 mm (black) above the table continuously for at least 0.10 s. Reference peak clearances are approximately 17.5 mm and 45.6 mm. The margins account for uncertain depth and edge orientation while separating a lift from sliding;0.10 s is three video frames. Neither object may be dropped to the floor.
  • During airborne manipulation (bottom clearance>5 mm), the object must have thumb contact and an opposing index or middle contact for >=80% of that interval, with >=90 deg separation of their contact-point vectors from the object center. The visible demonstration is a thumb–finger grasp throughout; reconstructed valid-contact opposition is near 100%. The 20% allowance covers contact switching and discrete physics contact reporting. There must be no uncontacted airborne interval longer than 0.10 s. Objects must be unconstrained free bodies moved by physical contacts, without scripted pose updates or attachment forces.
  • Each object must move at least 0.22 m toward+Y from its initial center, then finish with y>0.15 m. Measured reconstructed travel is about 0.273 m; the threshold reserves about 20% for the stated 10% geometry uncertainty and camera-motion sensitivity. Its blade axis must be within 20 deg of upright before its center crosses y=0.15 m: the demonstration turns each charger upright before the leftward carry finishes. The 20 deg tolerance covers uncertain intermediate rotation; the final upright requirement below is tighter. The instructional left/right boundary remains exact.
  • Each charger must be released onto its base with blade-axis tilt<=10 deg from world+Z. The instruction states blades up; reconstructed endpoints are constrained upright from visible blades and table support.10 deg is well within the approximately 24–25 deg static tipping angle of the specified bodies. Its lowest surface must be within 2 mm of the table, body-center linear speed<=0.02 m/s and angular speed<=0.2 rad/s, and it must have no hand contact for a final continuous 1.5 s. The video shows black unsupported for about 1.67 s and white for about 7.4 s;1.5 s preserves almost all of the shorter observation. The velocity and 2 mm thresholds distinguish settled support from transient contact or hovering at the contact-model scale.
  • White must remain upright on the left while black is manipulated, with <=5 mm drift from its released position. Both must satisfy the final standing conditions together. Final objects may not intersect each other; depth of any hand–object or object–table penetration must remain<=3 mm. Reference physical fits have sub 2 mm maximum hand–object penetration;3 mm permits rigid contact compliance, while excluding deep interpenetration.
  • The wrist must resemble the observed motion. Define human palm+Z opposite the wrist-to-middle-MCP direction, so palm-Z points toward the fingers; palm+X runs from pinky MCP toward index MCP projected perpendicular to Z, and palm+Y=Z cross X. The anatomical wrist joint is the palm-frame origin. Retarget to the robot mount using the inverse of the fixed mount-to-r_wrist transform from RIGHT_HAND.md (translation approximately[0.003,0,-0.028498]m, yaw 0.000016 rad), without mirroring. Use the fixed scene alignment in skill_measurements.json. Compare pose after linear time normalization within the measured approach, grasp, lift/turn, carry, place/release, return and withdrawal phases. Actual boundaries use sustained opposing contact; onset of lift/turn is rotation change>10 deg from the initial side pose or bottom clearance>5 mm, whichever occurs first; lift/turn completes when blade tilt<=20 deg and bottom clearance>5 mm both hold. Carry ends at the first supporting table contact after that point, which must be on the left; placement ends at final hand-contact break. Events last 0.05 s and final contact break 0.10 s. Apply the same geometric thresholds to the reconstruction: operational event frames are white 71/105/134/155/213 and black 254/282/326/360/385 (contact/turn onset/turn completion/near-support/release). These operational near-support times differ slightly from the visually annotated touchdown subgoals because 2 mm proximity precedes exact support. The measured records contain both. Empty or incorrectly ordered phases fail. Across supported reference samples, median position error must be<=0.06 m and 95th percentile<=0.10 m; median SO(3) rotation error<=25 deg and 95th percentile<=60 deg. No per-phase spatial fitting is allowed. The position tolerance is an explicit assumption covering different palm geometry and relative monocular depth uncertainty; the orientation tolerance covers the fitted wrist-prior discrepancy of about 7 deg, gravity-direction sensitivity of about 5 deg, and retargeting uncertainty. These require the demonstrated wrist approach, turning and carry poses, including the white grasp's near-down palm and black grasp's oblique palm. Measured phase poses/speeds are in skill_measurements.json. A 6 rad/s executed-target limiter introduces only about 0.32 deg peak reference lag in the final independent feasibility calculation.
  • The 20 finger joints must meet every hardware/SPECS.md position, speed and applied-torque rule at every 1 kHz physics/controller tick, with valid finite actions at the commanded interface. Any violation fails the episode. Wrist targets must use the specified impedance controller and rate limits. Initialize the wrist at the first supported demonstration hand frame 37; the corresponding remaining motion reference is 13.30 s. The episode must finish within 16.7 s, allowing about 25% for robot morphology/controller transients. Grasp-to-release spans must be 3.5–6.0 s for white and 3.2–5.6 s for black, around measured 4.73 s and 4.37 s with about 25% temporal tolerance.

Evaluation protocol and verdict

Evaluate 20 fixed, held-out seeds from the same declared scene, with independent uniform initial XY perturbations of +/-5 mm, yaw perturbations of +/-5 deg, and independent encoder noise as specified. Physical randomization, if any, is declared before use. These narrow perturbations test this demonstrated arrangement; they do not establish general dexterity. A passing policy must succeed in at least 18/20 episodes and have zero hardware-rule violations across all 20. Report each criterion per episode, aggregate success rate and uncertainty, checkpoint/hash, and representative successful and failed rollouts from top, left and right. Report the reference/base controller separately. A network must be trained with PPO, and the verified policy may observe only the deployable quantities in LEARNING.md.

These thresholds will not be relaxed to pass a policy. Any revision requires a new criteria version, reason, and separate verdict; old verdicts retain their original criteria.
