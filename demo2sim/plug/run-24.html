Verification criteria for plug manipulation

These criteria apply to the one white plug at every start in TASK_CONFIG.md section 4. The reference is reconstruction version 6, record r0009. The black adapter confirms the sequence in the demonstration and is absent in deployment. Frame-by-frame measurements and annotated images are under reconstruction/analysis/.

Ordered tests

  1. Lift. The thumb and another finger establish opposed contact on the housing, followed by an unsupported lift in the same grip. For the qualifying interval, the hand touches the plug while the plug touches neither the table nor another body. Its lowest point is at least 10 mm above the tabletop for at least 0.10 s, with the blades still more than 65° from vertical when the interval begins. The palm's outward normal points within 65° of down at lift onset. The gate also checks a rise of at least 15 mm above the start centre and at least 0.20 s total in opposed grip. The white reference from 3.50 to 3.77 s rises 15.9 mm at its centre and reaches 11.75 mm bottom clearance. It enters the turn with about 13 mm clearance. The inferred median palm normal is 4° from down during lift. The palm-angle tolerance accommodates the two hand morphologies and the robot's raised wrist clearance while preserving an approach from above.
  2. Turn. After lift, the blade axis reaches at most 25° from vertical in one uninterrupted opposed grip clear of the table, starting with tilt at least 65°. The blade axis in the palm frame rotates at least 38° within that turn. The world-space palm rotation over that interval is at most 50°. Across successive 100 ms windows in the turn, trail accumulated while the combined angular speed of the contacting digits' joints is at most 0.30 rad/s must be no more than 15°. Count thumb and whichever opposing digits contact during each window. The gate also checks a largest relative object rotation of at least 38° in an opposed grip and at most 15° of passive relative rotation with the standard measurements. The reference at 3.80–4.63 s changes blade tilt from 79.7° to 9.3°, with 52.1° of blade-axis turn relative to the wrist and 26.2° of wrist turn. Changing phase endpoints by a few frames gives 42.6–58.8° of relative turn. The 38° and 25° thresholds allow starting heading, depth, timing and embodiment differences. The 15° passive allowance covers brief contact transitions and excludes a wrist-only turn or a large slip.
  3. Carry. After the qualifying turn, the same manipulation enters the goal rectangle with the plug supported in the hand, clear of the table and with blade tilt at most 25°. Some translation during the turn is expected. The white reference moves 125 mm left from 4.40–5.17 s, finishing that segment with about 9 mm bottom clearance and 6.6° tilt. The required carry uses the deployment rectangle rather than the demonstration's absolute position. The gate checks at least 0.35 s total held off the table and final y in the left goal region.
  4. Place and release. Seat the plug onto the tabletop in the goal rectangle while the hand is still supporting it. The hand then separates and the episode ends under the given success condition. The white reference's back face is seated by about 5.50 s, and the thumb and fingers withdraw while the upright housing stays in place. The gate checks final x, y, z, blade-axis tilt and hand separation. The evaluator checks the entire final 0.5 s, including object velocities and robot encoder motion. Confirm no stop and no hardware violation over the episode, including the pre-start ramp.

A later retry may supply the ordered lift, turn, carry and seat. Judge each completed sequence from the same manipulation of the one plug. Derive clearance from the full collision shape and tabletop. Use the true simulated poses and contacts to measure the tests. Policy observations use the contract-v3 estimator and joint encoders. The mastery pass rate is 0.80, the task feasibility pass rate is 0.25, and both allow zero hardware violations.

Success condition (given; include it unchanged in your criteria):
- the plug's body centre is in the goal zone, x in [0.46, 0.56] m and y in [0.02, 0.12] m;
- it stands blades up: its +Z axis is within 10 deg of vertical;
- its centre is at its standing height on the table within +-10 mm;
- it is at rest: linear speed below 0.02 m/s and angular speed below 0.5 rad/s;
- all of this holds over the last 0.5 s of the episode, and the hand does not touch it then;
- the robot is at rest then too: over the same 0.5 s every finger joint moves less than 0.05 rad (max - min of its encoder reading) and the palm less than 5 mm.
