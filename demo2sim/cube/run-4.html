Criteria text

Own verification criteria — version 1

Written before simulation or PPO evaluation. These are my claims and tests, not official success criteria. Reference: reconstruction version 3, record r0008; TASK.md; research/recon_v3/NOTES.md and its measurements. A format-valid reconstruction is not evidence that a policy solves the task.

Task, evidence and ordered subgoals

The goal is to solve a legal 2×2 cube, retain it in the left hand throughout, and avoid breaking it. Deployment starts with the cube in the palm. The demonstration's timer operation, pickup and final placement are context, not required deployment actions.

  1. Receive and retain the roughly centered cube in a cupped starting pose chosen before training. Allow at most 0.25 s for passive placement settling, then establish thumb opposition.
  2. Inspect the allowed corner states, choose legal face turns, and alternate finger turns with regrasp/whole-cube reorientation when needed. Every motion command must come from a PPO-trained network. A finite symbolic move search/lookup using allowed states is permitted; physics or trajectory search at execution is not.
  3. Hold the solved cube stably for at least 0.5 s. Do not place it down to finish.

Video intervals (native frame k, real time k/30 s): grasp/lift79–97; orient/initial turns98–130; alternating turns131–201; regrasp/reorientation202–267; final turns268–341. Manipulation82–341 lasts8.667 s. Boundaries are visual estimates ±3 frames. Part turns and the exact move sequence are unresolved. The final two visible faces are monochromatic; hidden solved colors are inferred from the task, not measured. The set-down hand342–357 is unsupported by the fit and will not be used as a motion target.

Supported main-task frames:242/244. Measured palm-outward tilt from world up: min48.58°, p0559.33°, median74.15°, p95114.39°, max125.59°. Finger axis from up: min15.00°, p0534.41°, median65.17°, p9573.85°, max76.89°. Cube-to-wrist distance:0.112–0.154 m, median0.132 m. Wrist position spans approximately4–5 cm per axis. Opposed thumb/finger grasp fraction is about0.985 on supported contact frames. Contacts within occlusion are inferred geometrically, not directly observed.

Camera/fit uncertainty matters: bootstrap gravity-direction p952.24°, hand-normal sensitivity p955.37°, wrist-position sensitivity p9520.87 mm. Absolute metric depth sensitivity sigma45.6 mm is largely shared by hand and cube. Human and robot link shapes differ. These justify explicit geometric tolerances below, not arbitrary changes after policy evaluation.

Deployment distribution and fixed evaluation suite

The goal criteria apply to every legal short scramble and roughly centered placement described by TASK.md. For finite empirical verification, use a frozen suite of512 nominal episodes (seeds100000–100511) plus128 required stress episodes (200000–200127), disjoint from training seeds. Save every initial state and randomized parameter. A pass requires every episode in both suites to meet all applicable criteria; averages cannot hide drops or unsolved cases. Finite tests do not prove universal success on all continuous placements.

Nominal starts cover1–3 random quarter turns (both directions, including different axes), all six face-up choices, continuous yaw uniform[-π,π], palm-plane offsets independently uniform±15 mm, tilt up to15°, and release gap0–2 mm above the supporting palm. Include explicit cases across all24 cardinal cube orientations and each single face turn, not only random samples. Stress starts use4–5 turns, offsets±25 mm and tilt up to25°. Cube identity is fixed, its scramble/placement changes. A half turn counts as two quarter turns.

Uncertain object priors are tested: nominal size49–53 mm, mass45–70 g, sliding friction0.4–1.2, restitution0–0.15, and layer resistance0.0002–0.006 N·m. Stress extends size45–58 mm, mass40–85 g, friction0.25–1.4, restitution0–0.2 and layer resistance up to0.01 N·m. These ranges are explicit robustness assumptions around the manufacturer51 mm/55 g prior, not measurements. Record any mechanism-specific damping/detent ranges in the simulation before evaluation. Keep robot limits and specified controller gains authoritative. Encoder noise is exactly the hardware specification. No randomized physical parameter is supplied to the actor.

Episode limit30 s is an explicit engineering choice: it permits slower hardware-compatible turns/regrasps for short scrambles. It is not a measured human solve time. Supported reference wrist angular speed median0.88 rad/s,p953.93,max10.81 includes fit noise; target limit6 rad/s rules out exact peak replay. Preserve demonstrated orientations/contact roles with slower smooth transitions rather than demand an infeasible replay.

Goal and hardware criteria (every episode)

G1 — Solved state. All eight material corners must form a legal solved cube up to a global cube rotation. Compute from their known sticker-local axes and relative poses, not the planner's intended move sequence. Equivalently, minimize over the24 cube rotations G the maximum corner orientation error and position error against the canonical solved material-corner frames: rotation error≤3° and center error≤1.5 mm. All six faces must be monochromatic under that same G; no single-corner twist is allowed. Hold this state for0.5 s with root linear speed≤0.05 m/s and relative layer speed≤0.2 rad/s. The3° tolerance corresponds to about1.3 mm at a51 mm cube edge radius, comparable to rounded edges/pixel uncertainty; it cannot hide a wrong quarter turn.

G2 — Retention. Zero cube/floor contact from placement through completion; no other support or second hand. After the0.25 s placement settling allowance, at least one left-hand contact on≥99% of physics ticks, and no continuous contact gap>20 ms. Under gravity20 ms corresponds to about2 mm displacement, the contact/placement uncertainty, and does not permit a toss. Cube-center to wrist distance must remain0.07–0.18 m after settling. This surrounds the observed0.112–0.154 m with up to4 cm for receiving and differing link geometry. It must remain within the hand's reach throughout settling as well.

G3 — Integrity. Preserve eight connected material corners, their scale and sticker identities. No reset, replacement, teleport, direct object actuation, or solver-triggered object-state change during execution. The passive mechanism must penalize illegal independent corner motion. Fail if deformation away from a legal face-turn mechanism exceeds2 mm of corner-center displacement or8° of forbidden rotation for more than5 ms, or if hand/object penetration exceeds2 mm. These are conservative geometric integrity bounds around sub-millimeter nominal gaps and the reference penetration≤1.52 mm; fracture strength is unmeasured, so passing cannot establish real material safety.

G4 — Hardware. At every controller tick, all20 actual finger positions satisfy URDF limits±0.02 rad, actual velocities obey exact URDF speed limits, and applied torque obeys exact effort limits. Commands are finite with correct shape. Do not clamp simulated state to conceal a violation. Use the specified1 kHz PD/Cartesian impedance controllers, force/torque saturation, target speed limits and encoder noise. Check the free wrist and all self/object contacts for numerical instability. Any hardware violation fails the episode.

Skill criteria (after receive/settle, relative to the current cube)

S1 — Wrist style. During manipulation after the first1 s and before the final stable hold, palm-outward tilt from up must lie35–140° on≥95% of sampled control steps, and its episode median must lie59–115°. Finger-axis tilt from up must lie0–95° on≥95% of steps, with median34–74°. The broad instantaneous bounds add about15° to the measured extremes for gravity/fit uncertainty and robot morphology; the median bounds retain the demonstrated sideways/oblique wrist. A continually palm-up manipulation does not pass. Initial receiving may be palm-up. Evaluate only the manipulation interval; if solving completes in under1 s, use the post-grasp interval without discarding the episode.

S2 — Compact handling. Wrist displacement from the first established holding pose≤0.15 m throughout manipulation. The measured spans imply a total displacement scale below0.08 m; two21 mm fit uncertainties plus morphology allowance give the0.15 m bound. Cube-to-wrist distance must also satisfy G2. Global translation/yaw of the start is irrelevant; do not compare to fixed demonstration coordinates.

S3 — Grasp/contact roles. Thumb plus at least one opposing long finger must contact opposite sides of the cube on≥90% of manipulation steps; contact-point angle around the cube center≥60°. The reference has≈98.5% on supported frames; allowance covers discrete regrasp and different passive pads, without permitting sustained palm-only turning. For episodes with at least two completed layer turns, require at least one release-and-recontact by a non-thumb digit while another digit retains support. This follows the observed alternating finger turns and inferred contact transitions. Report individual finger contact fractions and relative fingertip travel; do not claim the exact hidden human contact sequence was reconstructed.

S4 — Contact-driven layer motion and adaptation. Every completed quarter turn must be produced by hand interaction with the passive articulated cube. Verify no hidden object actuator or goal-dependent physics is present. Use diverse body orientations/faces from the frozen suite; a network that holds or turns only the video's particular start fails G1 and the suite. Report turn success separately from cube reorientation and composed solve success.

Evidence, verdict and limits

For each evaluated PPO iteration record its simulation version, allowed observation/action interface, PPO architecture/hyperparameters/seeds, every reward and randomized quantity, curves and top/left/right videos. Report any scripted/reference baseline separately. The actor may observe only noisy joint encoders, wrist state/previous targets, cube/part poses and velocities, step index and constant task settings. Contacts, forces, rewards and physical parameters are not actor inputs. Check this interface in code.

Self-verification names the checkpoint hash, iteration record and these criteria version1; saves per-tick or lossless aggregated maxima/gaps, all per-episode criterion values, initial states and failure causes. Include representative successful and failed videos from all three views where present. Passing means G1–G4 and S1–S4 all met in all512+128 fixed episodes. A curriculum/one-turn success, mean reward, intended symbolic solution, or a rigid-object pose match is not a solve verdict.

Simulation evidence cannot establish real transfer, true fracture strength, exact hidden demonstration contacts, or all possible placements. The reference uses inferred physical priors and unresolved articulation. Report model approximations and these limits even if this empirical suite passes. Revisions require a new recorded criteria version with evidence and reason; never relax thresholds just to pass a policy.
