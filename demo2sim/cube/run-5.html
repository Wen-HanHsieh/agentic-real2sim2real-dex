Agent verification criteria, version2

These are my own simulation claims, not official task criteria or a ranking. TASK.md requires a one-handed, intact 2×2 solve from varied near-solved scrambles and placements, using the demonstrated manipulation and especially its wrist pose. A finite test cannot prove success for every possible placement or physical cube.

Evidence and interpretation

Reference: reconstruction6, recordr0010, from the395-frame source at real timek/30s. Source contacts span68–340 (boundary uncertainty±1frame); repeated turning/regrasp spans100–340,8.033s. The source picks up from a mat, releases near341, tumbles344–358 and rests359–394; deployment instead starts in the palm and must finish held. Pickup, timer operation and final release are therefore excluded execution goals.

Observed: left thumb support, changing opposing-finger contacts, index/finger flicks, regrasping and whole-cube/wrist reorientation. The final visible faces are uniform. Inferred: complete solved state, hidden contacts and metric properties. Exact source layer sequence and hidden stickers were not reconstructed. Six colors and the emblem break material symmetry, but the rigid geometric track retains a24-fold orientation ambiguity.

Final source estimates: left valid239/241turning frames; opposed grasp0.959, thumb contact0.963; maximum fitted hand-object overlap2.81mm; no detected finger self intersections. During turning, palm-normal elevation median18.87°,p05−21.21°,p9532.94°,range−29.50°to46.00°. Wrist-cube distance p05.10234m,median.11316m,p95.12137m. Wrist rotation relative to phase start has median14.04°,p9581.55°,maximum101.28°. Wrist angular speed p954.142rad/s,maximum7.089rad/s; linear maximum.389m/s. Absolute metric position sigma44–57mm includes camera/size/gravity uncertainty; wrist orientation sensitivity across fits is about7°p95. Among contact-consistent fits, wrist-position sensitivity is13mm p95, with an isolated79mm outlier.

Human wrist joint0 aligns to robot palm frame l_wrist, with+x toward thumb,+y back,+z away from fingers,palm normal−y. The commanded l_mount pose follows through the hardware fixed transform. Robot middle-tip joint-origin path.15600m divided by human middle-chain.1729m gives approximate morphology ratio0.90226. It informs a position tolerance, not a claim of exact retargeting. Literal sampled wrist speed exceeds the6rad/s executed-target cap: at least7.089/6=1.182time scaling would be needed; r0011 records this calculation. Timing may adapt while preserving poses and contact method.

Deployment distribution and assumptions

Each test starts with the same declared robot pose and one intact cube placed near the palm center, with no external support after release. Scrambles are legal sequences1–5quarter turns, excluding an already-solved result; “a few turns” is interpreted as this range before observing any policy performance. Length and face/direction vary. Initial orientation covers all24face-aligned rotations with continuous yaw/tilt, and uniformSO(3) orientations (including edge/corner-up starts). Palm-plane position varies±15mm in each horizontal coordinate. Height is set to place the lowest cube surface just above the hand without initial penetration; small release velocities are sampled up to.02m/s and.1rad/s. This is the operator placing/releasing the cube, not a robot grasp initializer or an attached object.

Nominal and perturbed tests cover edge49–53mm,mass45–70g,hand-object sliding friction.4–1.2,uncertain inertia and passive mechanism resistance/damping. The exact resistance ranges must be declared with the simulation before evaluation. Neither the policy nor a decision layer may observe those parameters, contacts,forces,rewards or images. Encoders use the specified noise. All starts are generated deterministically from suite seeds; training and test seeds are disjoint.

The test suite will use64fixed development seeds and128fresh final seeds, balanced among face-aligned,continuous orientation,placement-edge and physical-mismatch strata. Report every stratum, scramble length and failure. These finite ranges are explicit assumptions about imprecise placement and mismatch; they do not cover arbitrary abuse, severe damage or unbounded scrambles.

Ordered stages

  1. retain: For the first.5s after release, the cube stays within.18m of l_wrist, no cube-floor/external-object contact occurs, and all parts remain assembled. The.5s is an explicit settling/capture allowance; the source lift/grasp transition lasts about.7s. The.18m radius is larger than the robot's.156m finger-chain reach plus the cube half diagonal, and marks an unambiguous loss rather than a desired grasp distance.
  1. legal_turn: After retention, at least one complete legal90°layer turn (either sign,±5°alignment tolerance) is produced through physical hand contact while the opposite corners remain supported. Whole-cube rotation does not count. All four turned corners must form the same layer motion; no corner may be independently twisted or teleported into a solved permutation. The5°tolerance corresponds to about2.2mm edge displacement on a25.5mm radius and requires close alignment, rather than accepting a visibly part-turned cube. Turns may be selected by a discrete state-based solver, but every finger/wrist motion command must come from the PPO network. Object actuators, hand-object attachments and state snapping that supplies a turn are forbidden.
  1. solved: Within60s, all six faces have one color each, all eight corner identities/orientations are legal, and all layers align within5°. Evaluate actual part poses/colors, allowing a whole-cube rigid rotation; rotating a scrambled cube as a whole cannot satisfy this. The60s is an explicit practical episode assumption, not a time measured from the source; novel scrambles and slower hardware may require more than the demonstrated8.033s of turns. No floor contact,part separation or illegal transition may have occurred earlier.
  1. held_solved: Maintain solved alignment and one-handed support for1continuous second after reaching solved. Cube linear speed must be<.03m/s and angular speed<.3rad/s over the final.5s; those are explicit stability tolerances (at most15mm and8.6° over.5s), with orientation still constrained by the stricter5°alignment test. The source ends stable on a mat; deployment changes the supporting agent to the same left hand. There must be no external support at any time.

The last stage additionally requires the following skill and integrity conditions over the manipulation interval from the end of initial retention until solved, excluding the final static hold:

  • Wrist pose: Median palm-normal elevation within18.87°±15°; at least90%of samples inside[−36.21°,47.94°], the source p05/p95expanded by15°. This tolerance combines estimated wrist/gravity orientation error and robot-human retargeting differences; it is fixed before training. Persistent flat palm-up carrying at90°fails. Compute elevation asasin(world palm-normal·world up), from l_wrist axes, not a MANO parameter quaternion.
  • Full wrist inclination: The source finger axis (−z of the anatomical/palm frame) has elevation median22.18°,p0514.08°,p9549.78°; thumb-side axis(+x) has median57.68°,p0529.86°,p9570.95°. Require their manipulation medians inside[7.18°,37.18°]and[42.68°,72.68°], respectively, and at least90%of samples in[−0.92°,64.78°]and[14.86°,85.95°]. These are the same±15°orientation tolerance as the palm-normal criterion. Together they constrain wrist roll/pitch while leaving world yaw free to adapt to cube orientation. Compute axes from l_wrist; thumb-side-up is a visible feature of this left-handed demonstration. This condition is added before any policy evaluation because palm-normal elevation alone underconstrained wrist pose.
  • Wrist/object relationship: At least90%of samples have cube-center distance from l_wrist inside[.0723,.1295]m. This is the measured p05/p95distance multiplied by.90226, expanded by20mm for13mm contact-fit sensitivity and approximate morphology correspondence. The cube must be on the palm side and distal to the wrist: relativey<−.02m andz<−.03m in at least90%of samples. These sign/margin tests express the visible thumb/opposing-finger grasp and reject carrying behind the hand. No absolute camera-world trajectory is required.
  • Reorientation: For a scramble whose shortest legal discrete solution requires at least two face axes, the wrist's geodesic orientation range must reach at least15° and remain≤120°. The lower value is the source's approximately14°median excursion rounded to a measurable manipulation cue; the upper is its101°maximum plus orientation/retargeting tolerance. For a single-axis correction there is no minimum excursion; the same motion may adapt to a simpler start.
  • Contact method: Thumb and at least one opposing finger segment must lie within5mm of the cube on opposite sides for at least80%of manipulation samples, with thumb support for at least90%. Source fractions are.959and.963under a5mm reconstruction band. The margins account for discrete robot fingertip/segment geometry and brief support transitions during flick recovery, not a permitted drop. At least one non-thumb finger must break/re-establish contact or slide during a turn; the object cannot be solved by wrist motion alone.
  • Integrity: No floor contact,hand-object attachment,part detachment,illegal corner twist or numerical failure. No part may deviate>3mm from its permitted mechanism geometry; no hand-object penetration may exceed3mm in rollouts. The3mm tolerance is comparable to the final reference's2.81mm maximum and allows soft-pad/contact approximation without allowing fingers to pass through the cube. Record constraint/contact loads and deepest penetration. A rigid-mechanism model cannot prove a real cube will never fracture: material failure limits are unknown, and that limitation must appear in the verdict.
  • Hardware: Zero episodes with a finger position outside URDF bounds±.02rad, speed above its published limit, applied effort above its published limit, non-finite/wrong-shaped action or policy timeout. Wrist and finger controllers must match SPECS.md. Hardware violations invalidate success independently of task stages. Saturated PD demand is reported; it is not itself a violation when applied effort stays within limits.

Stages are reported as an ordered prefix; reaching an early stage does not waive later drop/integrity failures. held_solvedis the episode success stage only when all goal,skill,integrity and hardware conditions hold.
