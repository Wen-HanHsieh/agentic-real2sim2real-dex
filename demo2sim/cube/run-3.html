Own verification criteria v1 — D2S-CUBE-3

These are this run's claims about its simulation, not official evaluation criteria. Written after the full reconstruction stage and before any training scene or policy evaluation. Evidence: TASK.md; reconstructed version3/r0007; source395frames at realk/30s. The human manipulates with the left hand at68–341 and stops with visible yellow/green/orange faces uniform. Hidden final faces and the full layer sequence are unobserved; the instruction, not those hidden pixels, requires all six faces solved.

Deployment interpretation and scope

One intact2×2 cube is placed approximately in the middle of the chosen left-hand starting palm. The wrist and20finger starting positions will be explicitly fixed in the simulation record. The episode begins when the operator releases it. Any side may face up, with arbitrary yaw; uniformSO(3) orientations also test departures from face-up placement. “A few turns” means1–5quarter turns from solved, including inverse turns and half turns counted as2; reject accidentally solved starts. Six-turn scrambles are a stress case. This is an explicit interpretation of the instruction's unspecified scramble length.

Nominal geometry51mm/55g is an uncertain GAN-family prior, not measured ground truth. Required robustness range: edge48–54mm, mass45–70g, COM offsets≤2mm, positive-definite inertias0.6–1.4times the geometric estimate, pad/cube sliding friction0.35–1.2, and layer resistance0.002–0.030Nm. These broad ranges cover model mismatch; resistance is an engineering assumption because the video does not identify it. Mechanical contact/retention parameters and all randomization must be reported. Training may use a curriculum, but verification covers deployment starts independently of color scramble.

Placement core: center within10mm of the palm's chosen center; height0–15mm above a nonpenetrating supported placement. Stress:20mm horizontal displacement,20mm release height, and arbitrary orientation, with mass40–80g and edge47–55mm. “I won't always set it down exactly” motivates these stress placements. These finite ranges approximate the unbounded wording; results cannot prove performance for every real variation.

Ordered per-episode criteria

  1. Receive and retain in one hand. The cube never contacts the floor or any support other than the single left hand. Its center remains within120mm of the palm center. After a0.25s placement-settling interval, at least one cube–hand contact with positive normal force≥0.02N must exist, allowing no gap longer than10ms. The settling interval allows a20mm placement drop (about64ms under gravity) plus controller settling. The120mm containment radius covers the51mm cube's44mm half diagonal plus about75mm finger reach; actual contact and floor checks remain mandatory. The10ms allowance covers contact discretization at1kHz, not a deliberate toss. The source shows continuous support during68–341; its pickup/release onto the mat are excluded because deployment starts in-palm and requires retention throughout.
  1. Solve all six faces and finish there. At the fixed60s episode horizon, every face must have four matching colors, with all eight corner identities/orientations forming a legal solved cube up to a global proper rotation. Check the simulated labeled corner transforms, not just a rigid root pose, one face, a reward, or a face joint returning to zero. Every layer must be aligned within3deg of a legal quarter-turn detent and every corner within2mm of the corresponding assembled pose. A3deg error moves a face corner at36mm radius by1.9mm, matching the explicit2mm assembly-clearance tolerance. The solved state must persist for the final2s with retention and integrity still satisfied. This is roughly twice the source's final observed settled interval365–394 (0.97s), rejecting a momentary solved configuration crossed during continued turning.60s is an operational episode limit, around6times the human timer's9.49s, not a claim that the instruction imposed a speed requirement.

There is no privileged success-triggered termination or reset. The policy may use the allowed step index and stop moving on its own, but receives no color/solve signal. Final-window evaluation prevents a blind sequence from receiving an unavailable stopping cue.

  1. Remain intact and physically plausible. All eight corner pieces remain connected to the modeled interlock/core; no piece loss, illegal permutation, independent corner twist, duplicate occupancy, or persistent disassembly occurs. Corner-center radial error relative to the interlock's assembled radius must be≤2mm; legitimate layer rotations preserve that radius. Maximum hand–cube penetration is≤2mm throughout (soft pads/cover and finite-step compliance, compared with1.15mm in the reconstruction). Track and report the deepest penetration at every1ms physics tick. A conservative damage proxy fails an episode if an individual corner receives a contact/constraint load>20N or layer torque>0.25Nm; these are stated operating caps, not measured plastic strength.20N corresponds to about0.5Nm at a25mm lever and excludes excessive squeeze/jam solutions far above the assumed turning resistance. A model without fracture cannot establish real break resistance; the geometry/load proxy and this limitation must accompany any verdict.
  1. Respect hardware and the policy interface at every physics/controller step. Use the left URDF, its masses, limits, armatures and specified1kHz saturated PD controller. Joint positions stay within URDF limits±0.02rad, speeds never exceed URDF maxima, and applied torques never exceed effort limits. All commands are finite and correctly shaped. The floating wrist follows wrist-1 impedance gains, hand-only gravity compensation, force/torque caps and target rate limits. Report saturation as well as violations. Contact back-driving is included in velocity checks. A controller that teleports, overrides object motion or clips simulated velocities to conceal violations cannot pass.

The deployed network observes only noisy finger positions/velocities, wrist pose/velocity, its own previous targets, the single cube state-frame position/orientation/linear/angular velocity, step index and constant task settings. No corner/color state, face angles, contacts, forces, torques, random physical parameters, reward, simulator internals, episode seed or scramble ID may enter the actor. Its actions are finger position targets (optional velocity targets) and wrist pose targets through the specified controllers. Every RL stage uses PPO. Scripted/full-solve controllers and online search are not the reported policy; any baseline is evaluated separately.

Success requires all four criteria in order, in every evaluated required episode. Retention time, valid physical layer turns and color progress may diagnose failures but do not replace solved-and-held success.

Evaluation protocol and a passing verdict

Freeze checkpoints and evaluation seeds before evaluation, with physical/color randomization independent of observable initial poses. Required suite: all18one-face turns (six faces×±90/180deg) across24proper cube orientations (432cases);512seeded independent2–5turn scrambles with placement/physics variation;256six-turn/placement/physics stress cases. Include paired U/U-inverse scrambles with identical mechanical state, root pose and coupled encoder noise/randomness, testing the recorded observation-identifiability argument. If a simulator's coordinate mechanism represents some faces through whole-cube reorientation, record the equivalent generation without omitting labelings.

A passing verdict requires the entire suite completed with zero per-episode failures, plus deployable actor/interface and model-validation checks. This finite suite is evidence, not a proof over continuous starts. A failing verdict may stop a sweep after concrete counterexamples and must say which coverage was not run; diagnostic checkpoint evaluations can use smaller frozen subsets and cannot establish a pass. Report every initial state/seed, per-criterion metrics, representative top/left/right success and failure videos where present, actual sample count, and limitations. Report base-controller-only performance.

Outstanding ambiguity

A single cube root frame contains no sticker/corner labels. The recorded U/U-inverse symmetric-cube argument shows a possible indistinguishable pair; it will be tested rather than bypassed by adding observations or weakening the solved criterion. Body geometry, internal friction, break strength and camera/depth are uncertain. Any eventual verdict applies to the disclosed simulation and ranges, never to an official target or guaranteed real-world transfer. There are no target rounds in this task.
