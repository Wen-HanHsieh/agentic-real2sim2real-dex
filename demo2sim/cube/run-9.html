Agent verification criteria v1

These are my own simulation claims, not official success criteria. They apply to a single requested top-layer quarter turn, not a complete solve. I wrote them before evaluating a controller or policy. TASK.md decides the goal where the demonstration is ambiguous.

Evidence and uncertainty

The selected reference is research/recon_feature (final reconstruction record version4). Its format checker reports VALID and numeric validator passes, but five per-event rotation checks remain unresolved;18/395whole-object rotation rows are invalid. Complete hidden corner trajectories and the driving long finger are not recovered. The record tool permits an exception flag only for a failed format check; its refusal is recorded in r0014. This is a partial reference, not complete recovered motion.

Clear upper-layer turns are frames95–101,104–109,334–337 (real time k/30s). At95/101, constrained visible tile-corner fitting estimates +94.89degrees about cube z. With the corrected fitted wrist, the upper layer moves99.23degrees relative to the hand and the lower support14.47degrees over the0.2s endpoint interval. Independent planar-tile fits were ill-conditioned and are not reliable measurements. Exact intermediate angles remain unobserved. The axis is canted53.0->33.6degrees from vertical in those frames. Deployment instead selects the face on top at command time. Reverse direction comes from TASK.md; it is not established by these intervals.

Thumb bracing of lower/front tiles and a long-finger sweep are visible; rear contact and precise digit identity are partly hidden. Source hold69–341, release342, settling342–353. The task requires retention, so release is excluded. The corrected fit has94.1%opposed-grasp coverage and1.34mm maximum hand–cube penetration, conditional on its model. Palm-normal elevation has median21.73degrees and10–90%range13.64–29.12degrees; wrist-to-middle-MCP elevation median23.26degrees, range15.47–29.01degrees. Absolute depth has large camera/size-prior uncertainty and is not millimetre ground truth.

Deployment and sampling

The criterion applies to any legal, initially aligned2x2state, any side up, either requested direction, and placement roughly in the palm centre. The hand uses one fixed chosen start pose. We test all six up faces, continuous yaw, independent legal scrambles, horizontal placement offsets within10mm per axis, tilt within10degrees, and small placement-height variation settled onto the hand. These ranges operationalise "roughly in the middle"; they do not establish success outside them. Additional15mm/15degree starts are reported as stress tests when feasible. Starts must pass the supplied95%hold/support check with support=hand, not the floor. No start may rely on initial penetration or a falling cube.

Direction input is +1=counterclockwise, -1=clockwise as viewed from above the selected face. At command time choose the geometric face with maximal outward-normal dot world+Z; retain that face and its four corner identities for the episode. Scramble colours do not change the geometric goal.

Goal and mechanism measurements

Define a support frame from the nonturning lower four corners, preserving their initial identities. Express all corner poses relative to that frame. The goal applies a signed90degree rotation around the selected face normal through the cube mechanism centre to exactly the initial upper four corners; lower corners retain their initial relative poses. Evaluate individual identities, not orientation modulo cube symmetry. Rotating the entire cube therefore cannot satisfy the layer goal.

At completion require signed upper/lower angle within3degrees of the requested90degrees, cross-axis error at most3degrees, and every corner within5degrees and2mm of its counterfactual goal pose. This excludes wrong direction, wrong face,180/270degree substitutions, and a superficially identical envelope. Three degrees corresponds to approximately1.3mm edge displacement on a51mm cube;5degrees/2mm per corner accommodates numerical/compliance error while rejecting misaligned layers. All inactive layers must remain consistent with the same legal aligned cube.

Throughout, fit the corners to a valid single-layer-turn configuration (common support frame plus the selected continuous turn angle). Maximum part residual must stay below3mm and8degrees; penetration of a hand into the object must not exceed3mm. No broken, separated or independently tumbling pieces count as a turn. These are modelling-health assumptions, to be checked in state logs, not evidence that the hidden real mechanism is known.

Ordered stages

  1. gripped: cube supported in the hand for a contiguous0.30s, with thumb and at least one long finger in contact and no environment contact. This is the entry/settling primitive, based on the stable grip at136–153(0.567s) and the deployment's in-palm start.
  2. turned: the requested upper-layer goal above is reached while the cube remains retained. At least70degrees of progress must occur during contact with both the upper and lower portions and with an active long finger on the upper portion; thumb support on the lower portion must occur during the move. Total finger joint travel during the move must be at least0.25rad. At most15degrees may accumulate in0.10s windows where mean20-joint speed norm is below0.30rad/s (passive rotation). These are explicit physical discrimination assumptions based on the observed sweep and approximately95degree turn, not exact human joint-angle measurements. Correct direction and corner-identity goal are mandatory.
  3. settled: all goal and alignment conditions hold throughout a terminal0.50s; every part speed is below0.01m/s and angular speed below0.10rad/s. The cube still touches the hand and never touches the floor. These thresholds define "resting in your hand" and reject a transient angle crossing.

All stages must occur in order in an episode of at most8s, including settling. Eight seconds is an explicit practical control budget; the task prescribes no speed and I do not claim human-speed reproduction.

Global skill and hardware conditions

Hand contact must cover at least98%of the episode with no unsupported gap longer than20ms. This small tolerance addresses numerical contact chatter, not permission to toss the cube. Environment contact is forbidden at every tick. The cube must stay within0.18m of the wrist, a conservative anatomical reach envelope, and cannot be caught after a drop.

Palm outward-normal elevation must remain0–45degrees and the long-finger axis0–50degrees above horizontal. These include the measured ranges plus approximately15degrees for monocular uncertainty and hand morphology. Wrist rotation from its chosen start is at most25degrees, compared with approximately12.5degrees in the measured first turn; wrist displacement is at most0.05m, compared with approximately11mm at those endpoints. The goal comes first, but a different posture or a wrist-only move does not pass. Any needed hardware-related deviation requires evidence and a new criteria record, not silent threshold relaxation.

Every tick obeys hardware/SPECS.md and hardware/LEFT_HAND.md:20-joint position allowance0.02rad, exact speed and torque bounds, finite targets, specified1kHzcontrollers/500Hztarget interface and encoder noise. Zero episodes with a hardware violation are allowed in a passing gate. Actor observations are restricted to the deployable set in LEARNING.md; contacts and physical parameters are judge/training information only.

Suites, pass rate, and limits

Chain: grip from start, then alternative turn_cw or turn_ccw, each including alignment and retained terminal rest. Each link needs feasibility from actual handoff states at pass rate at least0.25, then its deployable skill suite at0.80. Each direction is a library skill; grip is its supporting primitive. The task suite reports gripped/turned/settled. Register fixed starts before training. Standard predicates include held/opposed duration, zero environment contact, final hand contact and wrist rotation; the frozen evaluator additionally checks the articulated-part and continuity criteria above from logged part states. Standard whole-object rotation is not substituted for articulation.

Use64distinct dev seeds, stratifying direction/up face/yaw/scramble; final evaluation uses128fresh gate seeds. Task pass requires at least0.90overall success, at least0.85in each direction, and at least0.80for every tested up-face subgroup, with zero hardware-violation episodes. Report counts and uncertainty, not only a mean. The90%threshold demands useful reliability while acknowledging research failures;80%is the required reusable-skill mastery threshold. Finite samples do not prove universal success.

Limitations: no target environment or real-robot trial exists. Object dimensions, mass, friction, compliance and hidden mechanics are priors. Success is meaningful only in the recorded simulator and its tested randomization, and must not be presented as an official or real-world result. Failed criteria remain failures; new evidence can motivate a separately recorded revision.
