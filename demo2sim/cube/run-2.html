Criteria text

Self-verification criteria v1

These are this experiment's own simulation claims, not an official evaluation. They are recorded before a training simulation or policy is evaluated. Reference: reconstruction v3, record r0009; task understanding in research/task_understanding.json; measurements in research/recon_v3/analysis/measurements.json and research/reference_handoff.json.

Scope and initial conditions

The instruction is to solve a 2x2 cube with one left hand. The human's exact scramble and individual layer turns are unobservable. This first claim concerns one fixed, known, nontrivial scramble, with perturbed grasp/physics trials; it does not claim arbitrary-scramble solving. Starting already held is an explicit initial-condition choice permitted by TASK_CONFIG.md. It uses source frame88 (2.933s real time), after pickup. No second hand, fixture, object actuator, weld to the hand, or external object assistance is permitted after reset.

Select the scramble deterministically before training: enumerate legal three-quarter-turn sequences in lexicographic order of cubie0-frame axis0,1,2 and sign-1,+1, excluding consecutive same-axis moves. Turn the face containing cubie0. Choose the first resulting configuration outside the complete zero/one/two-face-move neighborhood of solved (including half turns and rigid cube symmetry). Record the exact cubie rotations and sequence in the simulation record. This proves at least three face moves are necessary; it prevents an already solved or one-turn task. Geometry50mm and mass60g are specified, not fitted for success.

Reset only the physical robot/cube state into a documented retargeted grasp. At time0 all objects are free dynamic bodies and the policy/controller runs normally. No reset-time stabilization acts after time0. The 30 evaluation seeds are10000-10029 and are separate from training seeds. Each uses independent uniform perturbations: cube center +/-2mm per axis, cube global orientation +/-3deg rotation-vector components, wrist position +/-1mm per axis and orientation +/-2deg components, finger angles +/-0.005rad; joint targets remain clipped to specified limits. Encoder noise is the specified0.002rad/0.05rad/s. Finger-cube sliding friction is Uniform[0.8,1.2]; layer dry turning resistance Uniform[0.001,0.002]Nm. Other mechanism parameters, geometry and mass are the recorded nominal simulation values. The policy receives none of these hidden parameters.

Measurements and tolerances

The source is395frames at30real-fps,13.167s. Held manipulation88-336 spans8.30s; release/tumble337-354 spans0.60s; final rest355-394 spans1.33s. The selected held-to-end segment is307/30=10.233s. Use a10.24s episode (one50Hz command grid rounded upward by6.7ms, less than a source frame). Solving must finish before placement, by8.30s.

The reconstructed cube rises101.9mm from its initial rest center, reaches center height126.9mm above support, travels90.8mm horizontally from the selected held start during manipulation, and ends(-71.6,-17.5,-38.3)mm relative to that start. These metric values are conditional on the50mm proxy scale. Absolute depth has about68mm camera uncertainty; relative registered motion is better constrained. The held wrist peak speed is0.192m/s and angular speed4.625rad/s, below the hardware target limits. A local wrist basis is measured from MANO anatomy: +x toward the thumb (index MCP minus little MCP), -z wrist-to-middle MCP, y=z cross x. Left palm normal is-y.

Register demonstration W to simulation W once, with support atz0. Retarget wrist orientation with the anatomical basis above; apply a documented morphology correction to wrist translation and finger endpoints using the left robot dimensions. Freeze the reference and registration in the simulation record before training. No alignment is refitted on evaluation rollouts. Source-invalid hand frames remain excluded from wrist-error metrics; valid held-source coverage is238/249frames(95.6%). Reward interpolation across gaps is allowed only as an explicitly inferred training target.

The wrist tolerances below allow robot/human morphology and monocular fitting error:20mm is0.4cube edges;20deg corresponds to about34mm displacement at a100mm finger reach. They remain much smaller than the demonstrated wrist's full movement. The cube-center25mm tolerance is half an edge, accommodating regrasp offsets and uncertain monocular depth while requiring the same carry path. These are pre-policy assumptions, not tuned pass thresholds.

Ordered per-episode requirements

  1. Secure one-hand retention. For the first0.25s, the cube is retained in the left hand, with its lowest collision point at least5mm above the floor and center within40mm of its reset center. The source held start is about61.6mm center height, leaving roughly30mm corner clearance;5mm is a conservative collision-resolution margin. No floor-cube contact may occur before the cube is solved. All object external forces must be gravity/contact; passive internal mechanism forces are permitted.
  1. Legal dexterous solving. By8.30s the cube must be fully solved and remain solved for at least0.20s before release. All eight relative rotation indices must equal23, every cubie relative orientation must be within5deg of identity, and all centers within2mm of solved relative slots. The2mm derives from a21.65mm corner radius times5deg, about1.9mm. These are stricter than merely rounding to a24-rotation index. At least three legal face moves, involving at least two axes, must have completed. Whole-body rotation alone cannot satisfy this test. The independent episode audit replays logged legal layer commits from the initial state and checks continuous part poses against the allowed single-layer manifold. No cubie may exceed the specified15deg break limit in a successful episode; no detent or interlock violation may be hidden by resetting poses/velocities.
  1. Human-like wrist and object transport. From episode0 through8.30s, against the frozen retargeted reference, wrist angular error RMS<=20deg and95th percentile<=35deg; wrist-position error RMS<=20mm and95th percentile<=35mm; cube-center path RMS error<=25mm. Compute geodesic quaternion errors, with source frame88+30t, on source-valid wrist samples. Use the same fixed registration for object positions. During manipulation the thumb contacts the cube for>=90% of valid manipulation samples, and thumb plus an opposing long finger for>=65%, with surface gap<=2mm and contact-point opposition>=60deg. Reference MANO contact estimates are thumb97.7%, opposed69.4% using a5mm reconstruction band; the simulation uses a tighter2mm band because its surfaces are known. Brief regrips/cradle support are allowed. At least two long fingers must participate in contact at some time. No second manipulator or fixture may touch the cube.
  1. Solved placement and release. After solving, place/release onto the floor within25mm of the registered source final center. During the final1.00s, no hand-cube contact, all solved-state conditions remain true, lowest cubie point lies within1mm of the support (allowing compliant contact), center speed<0.02m/s and angular speed<0.2rad/s. Source final rest spans1.33s, with fitted4.75mm transient drift before the fully stationary end; the last second is essentially stationary. A dropped, broken or scrambled cube is a failure.
  1. Hardware and learning eligibility. Check all20 finger positions, velocities and applied torques at every1kHz physics/control tick against hardware/SPECS.md and LEFT_HAND.md: position allowance0.02rad, no speed/effort allowance; finite correctly shaped targets; specified effort saturation and controller rates. Do not classify wrist transients as finger hardware violations. The floating wrist uses the specified impedance, target speed limiting and force/torque saturation, without an added workspace constraint. No successful episode may violate hardware rules, and aggregate verification requires zero hardware violations across all30trials.

The final actor must be a closed-loop network trained with PPO, observing only noisy joint position/velocity, wrist pose/velocity, previous executed targets, cube state-frame pose/velocity, step index and fixed task constants. No per-cubie configuration, contact, force, reward, torque, hidden parameter, success flag, image or planner output is an actor observation. The fixed scramble is not an observation. A reference/residual controller is allowed, but its zero-residual performance must be reported on the same seeds. If that base already solves>=50% of trials, this protocol does not support presenting a script-dominated result as the learned solution; stronger causal evidence or a separately recorded criterion revision would be required.

Aggregate verdict and evidence

An episode passes only if all ordered requirements above pass. Overall PASS requires>=27/30episodes(90%) and zero hardware violations, with valid simulation/mechanism tests and a PPO checkpoint. Report every per-criterion value, uncertainty/limitation, failures, and representative top/left/right rollout videos. Preserve the initial cubie states, seeds, full controller/hardware maxima, legal-turn/break audit, wrist/path errors and final solved metrics. Baseline and PPO results are distinct. Failure requires diagnosis and another simulation/training iteration until the task's stopping rule holds. Any revision is a new recorded criteria version with a reason; no threshold is silently relaxed.
